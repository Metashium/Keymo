Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Keymo respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information and permissions.

1. Information Collection

Keymo does not collect, store, or sell personal information.

The app does not require user accounts or cloud-based sign-in.

2. Local Network Communication

Keymo communicates with devices on your local network in order to provide wireless remote control functionality.

Communication occurs directly between your devices and is not routed through external servers operated by Keymo.

3. Permissions

Keymo may request permissions required for app functionality, including:

  • Network access for device communication
  • Vibration access for haptic feedback
  • Storage or media access for wallpapers or customization features

4. Third-Party Services

Keymo may use third-party services such as Google Play Billing for in-app purchases.

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

Keymo is designed to operate within local networks and requires user interaction to establish device connections.

6. Children's Privacy

Keymo is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Changes will be posted on this page.
